🚀 My Journey: From Network Infrastructure to Software Engineering

🌐 The Foundation: Enterprise Networking

My journey in technology began at Subisu Cablenet in Kathmandu, where I worked as a Technical Assistant. This role completely immersed me in the world of enterprise-level networking, placing me on the front lines of diagnosing and resolving complex system issues.

Working directly with critical infrastructure gave me a profound understanding of the digital backbone supporting today’s technology-driven world. More importantly, it fundamentally shaped how I approach technical challenges:

  • Engineering-First Mindset: Gained deep operational insights into system reliability, scalability, and performance.
  • The Troubleshooting Blueprint: Developed strong analytical and problem-solving skills under high-stakes environments.
  • Precision Under Pressure: Built a detail-oriented habit that translates directly into writing clean, optimized, and efficient code today.

⚡ The Pivot: Choosing to Build, Not Just Support

While maintaining and optimizing enterprise architectures gave me an invaluable technical foundation, I gradually realized my true calling: I wanted to create impactful digital solutions, not just support existing ones.

Driven by this ambition, I made a conscious transition into software engineering and pursued an intensive diploma in Mobile App Development at Broadway Infosys. This pivotal phase allowed me to sharpen my programming skills, master modern software architectures, and discover the Flutter framework. Flutter immediately captivated me with its unique ability to craft visually stunning, high-performance cross-platform applications from a single codebase—opening the door to the creative, innovative side of software development I had always wanted to explore.

💻 What I Do Today

As a software developer based in Kathmandu, I bring a unique edge to my projects by bridging the gap between infrastructure reliability and modern application design. I think beyond just writing code—I build well-rounded digital products with an architectural understanding of both backend power and frontend fluidity.

Areas of Specialization:

  • Cross-Platform Mobile Apps: Building responsive, seamless, and high-fidelity user interfaces using Flutter & Dart.
  • Backend & Architecture: Designing stable, scalable, and maintainable data architectures using Java & MySQL/JDBC.
  • Full-Stack Synergy: Connecting robust database management with intuitive frontend layouts to deliver airtight user experiences.

My Ultimate Goal: To design efficient, reliable, and user-centric technology solutions that solve real-world problems and create genuinely meaningful digital experiences.
